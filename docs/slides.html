Online News Popularity: Predicting Article Sharing

Course: Introduction to Machine Learning (Project Lab) · Group: G11

Core Question: Can an editor predict social shares before publication using article text statistics, media assets, LDA topics, publishing day, and historical keyword performance?

Dual Formulations

  • Classification: Predict binary popularity ($shares \ge 1,400$, training median; answers "prominent slot?").

Methodological Rigor & Protocol

  • Leak-Free Holdout Split: 80% Train (31,715) / 20% Test (7,929), seed=42. Scalers fit only on train. Threshold = training median (1,400).
  • Pruned Columns: Dropped url (ID) and timedelta (prevents memorizing calendar drift).
  • Empirical Proof for Log-Target:
    TargetMAE (shares)Median Error (shares)Log R²
    Raw shares2,998.71,682.3-0.3898
    Log(shares)2,347.8 (-22%)733.1 (-56%)+0.1274

Model Interpretability & Feature Drivers

  • Permutation Feature Importance (Held-out Test):
    • kw_avg_avg (+0.0565 ΔR²): historical average shares of article keywords.
    • kw_max_avg (+0.0196) and self_reference_avg_sharess (+0.0172).
  • Signal Distribution:
    • 31.9% Historical past performance (kw_*, self_reference_*).
    • 33.5% Content & links | 15.0% LDA topics | 12.9% Sentiment.
  • Core Takeaway: The model is predicting the past forward—it recognizes topics that audience previously shared, rather than judging writing brilliance.
